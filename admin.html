How Publish works

Photo changes are staged in the editor. Save a photo and it appears here right away, but visitors keep seeing the previous photo until a rebuild has run.

In short: this Publish button has a quota of 500 uses per month. You probably will not reach it. Each Publish starts a Cloudflare Pages rebuild, so stage and save all your photo changes, review them, then publish once. The rebuild usually takes about a minute and runs on its own.

Only photo changes require Publish. Text changes do not. Text, menu, hours, and other non-photo content can be changed and saved independently. They do not use a photo rebuild. For text changes, just press Save changes at the bottom right.
