01 The Idea

Georgian cooking for a Manhattan morning.

Aromati, from the Georgian word for aroma, is a bi-level café and wine bar in Murray Hill, where American coffee culture meets the oldest food and wine traditions in Europe.

Downstairs, an open kitchen and coffee bar move at the pace of the city: espresso pulled, matcha whisked, khachapuri blistering in the oven. Upstairs, the room slows down into burgundy walls, tufted leather, cane and oak, and low globe light, a dining room and wine bar built for long evenings.

04 The Wine Bar

A wine list shaped by Georgia.

Upstairs, the list brings together qvevri ambers, Saperavi, natural wines, and a short European selection. Wines are available by the glass or bottle, with pairings for the table.

  1. I
    Amber

    Amber wines bring notes of honey, spice, dried fruit, and citrus.

  2. II
    Saperavi

    Saperavi is a Georgian red with dark berry, plum, and cherry notes.

  3. III
    Qvevri

    The list includes qvevri wines in both amber and red styles.
